Our Offering

We Provide an Energy Solution Across All Sectors

Since 2015, Sun Exchange has developed and delivered more than 100 solar projects across South Africa. These projects span schools, farms, businesses, retirement communities, and other high-impact organisations. Each project reduces electricity costs, provides reliable power, and creates measurable environmental and social benefits.

To help you find the right fit, explore the sector that best matches your organisation. We’ll guide you through the tailored solar solution designed to meet your needs.

Education

Schools, universities, and training centres face rising energy costs and frequent power disruptions. Solar allows these institutions to lower operating costs while ensuring a stable supply of electricity for classrooms, labs, and facilities. Educational dashboards also let students learn from real-time solar data, making the system both a financial and teaching tool.

Agriculture

Farms and agri-businesses need consistent, affordable energy for irrigation, refrigeration, and processing. Diesel generators are costly and harmful to the environment, while grid power is often unstable. Solar systems provide dependable energy during peak daylight hours and can be paired with storage for round-the-clock resilience.

Property or Business Owners

Commercial properties, office buildings, and retail spaces require efficient energy to keep businesses running. Rooftop solar solutions cut overhead costs, improve property value, and demonstrate sustainability leadership to tenants and customers. With Sun Exchange’s risk-free model, property owners only pay for electricity produced.

Retirement & Lifestyle Estates

Lifestyle estates and retirement communities depend on consistent power for comfort, safety, and essential services. Solar provides reliable, affordable energy that reduces monthly costs while creating a healthier, greener environment for residents. With all maintenance and insurance managed by Sun Exchange, estates enjoy the benefits without the hassle.
