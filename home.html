Commercial Rooftop Systems

Small and medium-sized businesses often face rising grid tariffs, unpredictable load shedding, and high operational costs during peak demand hours. Commercial rooftop solar provides a direct way to stabilise energy costs while demonstrating a commitment to sustainability.

Key Benefits

  1. Daytime optimisation:Systems are designed for peak daytime loads when most businesses operate.
  2. Scalable capacity:From small retail outlets to multi-site office buildings, arrays can be sized to match demand and expanded later.
  3. Quick payback:Savings are immediate when a high percentage of the generated electricity is consumed on-site
  4. Resilience:Paired with batteries, systems reduce downtime during grid failures
  5. Brand value:Demonstrates environmental responsibility to customers and investors

Institutional & Education Systems

Schools, clinics, and public institutions are highly dependent on reliable power but often constrained by budgets. Solar systems reduce operating costs while ensuring critical services remain uninterrupted.

Community Solar Projects

In townships, peri-urban areas, and rural communities, individual households may not have the means to finance solar on their own. Community solar projects aggregate demand, pool resources, and extend clean energy access to underserved groups.

Solar Panels


Solar panels are central to the shift toward clean, renewable energy. They transform sunlight into electricity, reducing dependence on fossil fuels and providing long-term environmental and financial benefits.
