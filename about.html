About Us

A Trusted Leader in Solar

Established in Cape Town in 2015, Sun Exchange has grown into a recognised leader in distributed solar energy solutions across South Africa. Over the past decade, we have made clean, reliable, and affordable electricity accessible to more than 100 host sites, including schools, farms, retirement homes, nonprofits, and other high-impact organisations that form the backbone of our communities. Our model is built on trust and simplicity. We take on every element of the process that would otherwise be a barrier to adopting solar. This includes financing, system design, equipment selection, installation, insurance, monitoring, operations, and ongoing maintenance. By removing these risks and responsibilities, we ensure that our clients only focus on what matters most: benefiting from dependable, low-cost solar energy.


With Sun Exchange, you do not need to worry about technical expertise, hidden costs, or long payback periods. You simply pay for the electricity generated by the system, starting from day one. The result is immediate savings, predictable energy costs, and the knowledge that your organisation is actively reducing its carbon footprint. Beyond financial savings, our projects deliver measurable social and environmental impact. Every installation helps reduce reliance on fossil fuels, cuts greenhouse gas emissions, and supports South Africa’s just transition to renewable energy. At the same time, local installers and technicians benefit from job creation and skills development, ensuring our projects uplift the communities they serve. Sun Exchange continues to lead with innovation, transparency, and a commitment to sustainability. By choosing us, you join a growing movement of organisations and individuals who are powering Africa’s future with clean energy, one solar panel at a time.

Mission

Sun Exchange removes the barriers to solar. We raise project finance. We design and build the system with certified partners. We insure and monitor performance. We run maintenance and handle repairs. Hosts never pay for hardware. They pay a fair rate per kWh. That means instant savings and predictable energy costs. Investors fund the panels and earn steady returns. Communities gain reliable clean power. Everyone benefits.


We make solar simple and risk free. We finance, install, insure and maintain systems. You pay only for the clean electricity they produce.

  • No upfront capital for hosts
  • End to end project delivery
  • System insurance from day one
  • 24/7 remote monitoring and fast repairs
  • Pay per kWh billing with clear invoicing
  • Transparent performance reports and dashboards
